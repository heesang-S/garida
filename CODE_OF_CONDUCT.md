# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community for everyone who
participates in Garida, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, ask questions in good faith, and focus feedback on ideas
  and code rather than people.
- Respect differing technical opinions and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect others' privacy and do not share private information without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized
language or imagery, doxxing, and disruptive bad-faith behavior are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness a violation, use the repository's private
[security-advisory reporting channel](https://github.com/heesang-S/garida/security/advisories/new)
and clearly state that the report concerns conduct. Maintainers will review
reports privately and may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict
participation as appropriate.
